### 2. Preparation Journey

**"Walk me through how you prepared. What resources did you use?"**

I used the cases for paces book.
I attended the paces ahead course.
The Walton centre neuro paces course.
I attended some online webinars run by intelligent paces.
I also attended weekly in person paces group classes which was lead by a consultant.
On car journeys I would listen to the “pre paces podcast”
I used YouTube videos to get examination tips.

I bought the pastiest video bank but only watched 25% of the videos as I found it more helpful to practice in person.

I used chatgpt to give me cases and ask me questions as though I was in the consultation station.


**"What was your daily/weekly study routine like?"**

I did in person paces practice 2-3X a week. I worked with my paces buddy to find patients and examine them.
We would take it in turns to examine and then present our findings with each other.
We would use the cases for paces book to question / viva each other.
We would check the patient’s examination findings in the patient’s notes and letters.


**"How much did you spend on courses, books, question banks?"**

£1000 for paces ahead course
£400 neuropaces course
£30 cases for paces book.
£200 pastiest resource.


**"What was the single most valuable thing you did to prepare?"**

Examine patients and practice presenting.


**"What was the biggest waste of time or money?"**

Pastest video bank.
