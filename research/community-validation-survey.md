## Part 1: Reddit & Community Deep-Dive

### Key Pain Points from Community Discussions

#### 1. **Books + Videos Aren't Enough**
- Multiple candidates report failing PACES despite "reading clinical examination books and watching examination videos"
- One UK trainee failed twice by 4 marks each time using only books and online resources
- **Quote:** "The books and online resources clearly weren't working. I signed up for a 'Focus on Neurology for PACES' course and an expensive 3 day PACES revision course."
- **Insight:** Candidates are spending significant money on in-person courses because digital resources alone don't prepare them adequately

#### 2. **Lack of Structured Practice**
- No PACES-specific question bank exists for digital practice
- Candidates rely on generic medical Q-banks (Pastest, iatroX) which are written-exam focused
- **Quote:** "Two hours of reading about cardiovascular examination will not make you better at cardiovascular examination, but fifty patients examined under time pressure will."
- **Insight:** The gap between knowledge and performance is the core problem

#### 3. **IMGs Face Unique Challenges**
- Limited access to ward environments and PACES teaching groups
- Need longer preparation time (6+ months vs 3 months for UK trainees)
- **Quote:** "IMGs typically need longer. This is purely because they have more limited access to ward environments and PACES teaching groups, which form the backbone of solid preparation."
- **Insight:** IMGs are a large, underserved market segment

#### 4. **Fragmented Resources**
- Candidates juggle multiple resources: Pastest (written), in-person courses, YouTube, books, College resources
- No single platform integrates PACES-specific preparation
- **Quote:** "Oncourse AI is the best modern study layer for MRCP PACES preparation resources 2026 because PACES is won by repeated clinical reasoning, station practice, and weak-skill repair, not by collecting another untouched book or course."
- **Insight:** There's demand for an integrated solution

#### 5. **Time Pressure & Rota Constraints**
- Working doctors have no fixed calendar — revision must happen in 30-45 minute fragments
- **Quote:** "The binding constraint on MRCP, MRCGP, MRCEM or SCE preparation is not intelligence or resources; it is that revision must happen in 30 to 45 minute fragments around a rota that does not care about your exam date."
- **Insight:** Mobile-first, fragment-friendly design is essential

#### 6. **High Cost of In-Person Courses**
- PACES courses cost £500-2000+ for 3-4 day intensive programs
- Many candidates fail even after attending expensive courses
- **Insight:** There's a market for affordable digital alternatives that provide structured practice

### What Candidates Wish They Knew Earlier

1. **Start with free resources first** — Royal College materials, Pastest blog guides
2. **Practice with real patients under time pressure** — not just reading
3. **In-person practice is irreplaceable** — but expensive and hard to access for IMGs
4. **Don't measure prep in hours — measure in patients examined**
5. **Failed candidates often attribute failure to lack of structured practice, not lack of knowledge**
