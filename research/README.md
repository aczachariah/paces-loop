## Research conventions

- Preserve source transcripts separately from interpretation; do not silently rewrite participant wording.
- Record provenance, participant relationship and sample limitations.
- Distinguish participant statements, product hypotheses and verified external facts.
- Candidate usability interviews do not replace qualified clinical review.
- Hypothetical interest or pricing answers are not observed demand or educational outcomes.
- Revalidate competitor features and official examination requirements before relying on them.
