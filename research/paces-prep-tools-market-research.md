## Executive Summary

The PACES (Practical Assessment of Clinical Examination Skills) exam prep market is **fragmented and underserved** compared to USMLE or PLAB prep. There is no dominant "UWorld for PACES" — the landscape is dominated by:

1. **Pastest** — the closest thing to a market leader (question bank + courses)
2. **In-person courses** — the primary preparation method for most candidates
3. **Free/low-cost resources** — guides, YouTube, Reddit communities
4. **General medical study tools** — iatroX, Geeky Medics (OSCE-focused)

**Key gap:** No dedicated PACES-specific digital question bank with adaptive learning, spaced repetition, and clinical scenario practice exists. Most candidates rely on in-person practice stations and generic medical study tools.

## Existing Tools & Platforms

### 1. Pastest
- **URL:** https://www.pastest.com
- **Type:** Question bank + online courses + PACES-specific content
- **PACES relevance:** HIGH — has dedicated PACES blog, guides, and likely PACES question bank
- **Strengths:**
  - Most established brand in MRCP/PACES prep
  - Dedicated PACES preparation guides (3-6 month timelines)
  - Question bank for MRCP Part 1 and Part 2 Written
  - Active community and blog content
- **Weaknesses:**
  - Primarily focused on written exams (Part 1/2)
  - Limited evidence of PACES-specific digital practice tools
  - No adaptive learning or spaced repetition for clinical skills
- **Pricing:** Subscription-based (exact pricing not publicly listed in search results)

### 2. iatroX
- **URL:** https://www.iatrox.com
- **Type:** AI-powered medical study platform with Socratic Tutor
- **PACES relevance:** MEDIUM — UK-focused, covers postgraduate exams
- **Strengths:**
  - Socratic Tutor on missed questions (asks reasoning before revealing answers)
  - Adaptive sequencing and spaced repetition built into the bank
  - £99 annual subscription spans multiple exams (Royal College, 13 SCEs)

  - Not PACES-specific
  - Focused on written exams, not clinical skills

## Reddit Community Insights

### Key Sources Found:
- r/ukmedicalgraduates (primary community for UK medical graduates)
- r/MRCP (MRCP-specific discussions)
- Pastest community discussions
- IMG Connect forums

### Common Themes from Search Results:
1. **PACES preparation typically takes 3-6 months** — most candidates start 3 months out, ideally 6
2. **In-person practice is king** — candidates emphasize practicing with real patients under time pressure over reading
3. **Pastest is the go-to digital resource** — mentioned as the primary online preparation tool
4. **Clinical exposure is critical** — "fifty patients examined under time pressure" beats "two hours of reading"
5. **IMGs need more time** — international medical graduates typically need longer prep due to limited ward access
6. **No dedicated PACES question bank exists** — candidates rely on generic medical Q-banks + in-person practice

### Notable Quotes/Insights:
- "Don't measure your preparation in hours. Measure it in patients examined."
- "Two hours of reading about cardiovascular examination will not make you better at cardiovascular examination, but fifty patients examined under time pressure will."
- Most candidates use a combination of: Pastest (written exam prep) + in-person practice stations + generic medical resources

## Market Gaps & Opportunities

### 1. **Dedicated PACES Question Bank (Digital)**
- **Gap:** No digital question bank specifically for PACES clinical scenarios
- **Opportunity:** Create a PACES-specific Q-bank with:
  - Clinical vignettes mapped to PACES stations
  - Mark scheme-aligned feedback
  - Adaptive learning based on performance
  - Spaced repetition for clinical findings

### 2. **Virtual PACES Practice Platform**
- **Gap:** No virtual patient platform for PACES-style stations
- **Opportunity:** AI-powered virtual patients for:
  - History taking practice
  - Examination technique feedback
  - Communication skills assessment
  - Real-time mark scheme scoring

### 3. **PACES-Specific Analytics**
- **Gap:** No platform tracks PACES-specific performance metrics
- **Opportunity:** Analytics dashboard showing:
  - Strengths/weaknesses by station type
  - Progress over time
  - Comparison to peer performance
  - Predicted PACES score

### 4. **Mobile-First PACES Prep**
- **Gap:** Most resources are desktop/web-based
- **Opportunity:** Mobile app for:
  - Quick clinical finding reviews
  - Mini-mock stations during commutes
  - Spaced repetition for clinical signs
  - Offline access for ward-based study

### 5. **Community & Peer Practice**
- **Gap:** Limited digital community for PACES candidates
- **Opportunity:** Platform features for:
  - Partner matching for practice
  - Peer feedback on clinical skills
  - Shared study schedules
  - Success stories and tips
