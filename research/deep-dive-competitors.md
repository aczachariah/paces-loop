## 1. Pastest — The Incumbent

### Overview
- **URL:** https://www.pastest.com
- **Type:** Medical exam question bank platform
- **Scale:** Trusted by 1,000,000+ medical students and doctors
- **Data:** Based on over 3,000 post-exam survey datapoints
- **Positioning:** "Your one-stop shop" for medical exam prep

### PACES-Specific Offerings
Pastest does NOT have a dedicated PACES question bank. Their PACES relevance is **LOW**:
- They cover MRCP Part 1 Written and Part 2 Written extensively
- PACES is mentioned in eligibility/content but not as a primary product
- Their strength is written exam preparation, not clinical skills

### Pricing Model
- **Per-sitting subscription:** £95–£180 per exam sitting
- **Multi-exam bundles:** Available for doctors sitting multiple exams
- **No PACES-specific pricing** — PACES candidates typically bundle with written exam prep

### Key Features
1. **Structured Learning** — Understand clearly with comprehensive explanations
2. **Practice Under Pressure** — Timed exam simulations
3. **Performance Insight** — Identify weaknesses honestly
4. **Reinforcement** — Strengthen consistency and retention
5. **Progress Tracking** — Monitor improvement over time

### Strengths
- **Massive user base** (1M+) — strong brand recognition
- **3,000+ post-exam survey datapoints** — data-driven question design
- **Polished platform** — reliable, well-maintained
- **Multi-exam coverage** — MRCP Part 1, Part 2, MRCP PACES (limited), MRCGP, MRCEM
- **50+ years of reputation** — established trust in medical education

### Weaknesses
- **Not PACES-specific** — no dedicated clinical skills question bank
- **Static by design** — limited adaptive learning for clinical skills
- **Written exam focused** — PACES is an afterthought
- **Expensive** — £95-£180 per sitting adds up for serial exam takers
- **No clinical practice simulation** — can't practice examination skills digitally

### Business Model
- **Subscription-based** — per-exam sitting pricing
- **High customer lifetime value** — doctors sit multiple exams over career
- **Network effects** — large user base → more survey data → better questions → more users
- **B2C direct** — no institutional licensing mentioned

## 2. Geeky Medics — The Clinical Skills Leader

### Overview
- **URL:** https://app.geekymedics.com
- **Type:** Free OSCE stations, AI patients, medical MCQs, flashcards, notes
- **Positioning:** Free clinical skills education platform
- **Pricing:** £69.99/year for "Everything Bundle"

### Business Model & Monetization

**Freemium Model:**
- **Free tier:** Access to basic OSCE stations, checklists, and notes
- **Premium tier (£69.99/year):** Everything Bundle — full access to all features

**Revenue Streams:**
1. **Subscription revenue** — £69.99/year for Everything Bundle
2. **High perceived value** — "Everything" implies all features included
3. **Low churn** — clinical reference material is sticky (used throughout training)

**Key Insight from iatroX Analysis:**
> "UK medical student, UKMLA year. Stack version: Geeky Medics Everything Bundle (£69.99 per year) for CPSA stations, plus a written-exam bank, plus ChatGPT for explanation. Consolidated version: iatroX (£99 per year) covering the AKT bank, adaptive revision, Tutor, planner and mocks, with Geeky Medics added for the clinical exam window. Either way you land near £170 per year."

This reveals:
- **Geeky Medics is often ADD-ON to other platforms** — not a standalone solution
- **Candidates stack multiple subscriptions** — total cost ~£170/year
- **Geeky Medics is the "clinical skills" layer** in most stacks
- **Free tier drives adoption**, premium converts power users

### Features
1. **1,300+ OSCE stations** — comprehensive clinical skills library
2. **Virtual patients** — interactive clinical scenarios
3. **AI examiner feedback** — automated assessment of clinical skills
4. **Checklists** — structured examination guides
5. **Notes & flashcards** — revision materials
6. **Medical MCQs** — written exam practice (secondary feature)

### Strengths
- **Free tier drives massive adoption** — no barrier to entry
- **Best-in-class clinical skills content** — "nothing else on this list is close for consultation practice"
- **Virtual patients** — unique differentiator for clinical skills practice
- **AI examiner feedback** — automated assessment (rare in clinical skills)
- **1,300+ OSCE stations** — largest free library
- **Low price point** — £69.99/year is affordable
- **Sticky product** — used throughout medical training, not just exam prep

### Weaknesses
- **Not PACES-specific** — general OSCE focus, not MRCP PACES
- **Often an add-on** — candidates stack it with written exam banks
- **Limited adaptive learning** — static content, no personalized learning path
- **No spaced repetition** — doesn't reinforce weak areas automatically
- **Fragmented analytics** — performance data doesn't persist after graduation
- **Switching friction** — candidates use multiple apps, each with separate logins

### Monetization Insights
- **£69.99/year is the sweet spot** for clinical skills tools
- **Freemium works** — free tier drives adoption, premium converts
- **Bundle pricing** — "Everything Bundle" implies maximum value
- **Low churn expected** — clinical reference material is always useful
- **Cross-sell opportunity** — candidates already spending £170+/year on stacked tools

## Key Strategic Insights

### 1. The Market is Fragmented
- **No single tool covers all needs** — candidates stack 2-3 subscriptions
- **Total cost for candidates:** ~£170-300/year when stacking
- **Opportunity:** Consolidate into one platform

### 2. Pricing Sweet Spots
- **Pastest:** £95-180/sitting (expensive, per-exam)
- **Geeky Medics:** £69.99/year (affordable, annual)
- **PACES AID:** £95-215 one-time (no recurring)
- **Oncourse AI:** ~$470/year (based on $5.6k/mo revenue)
- **Your opportunity:** £50-150/year range with adaptive learning

### 3. The Core Gap
- **No tool combines:** PACES-specific + adaptive learning + clinical skills practice + AI feedback
- **PACES AID** is PACES-specific but lacks adaptive learning
- **Geeky Medics** has clinical skills but isn't PACES-specific
- **Oncourse AI** has adaptive learning but limited PACES depth
- **Pastest** has scale but isn't PACES-focused

### 4. Monetization Patterns
- **Freemium works** — Geeky Medics drives adoption with free tier
- **One-time payment appeals** — PACES AID's model is popular
- **Subscription preferred** — most tools use recurring revenue
- **Bundle pricing** — "Everything Bundle" implies maximum value
- **Annual pricing** — £50-150/year is the expected range

### 5. Feature Priorities (from research)
1. **PACES-specific content** — non-negotiable
2. **Adaptive learning** — differentiator (only Oncourse AI has it)
3. **Clinical skills practice** — virtual patients, AI feedback
4. **Spaced repetition** — reinforces weak areas
5. **Mobile-first** — study on wards, during commutes
6. **Offline access** — important for ward-based study
7. **Community features** — peer practice, discussion
